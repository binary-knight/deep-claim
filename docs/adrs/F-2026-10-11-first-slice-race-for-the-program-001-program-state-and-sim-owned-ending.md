# ADR-F-2026-10-11-first-slice-race-for-the-program-001: The program is company state; the sim decides when the game has ended

**Date:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** Accepted
**Context:** The game gains a goal and an end (spec BR-001 to BR-009). Today a game ends only when the session sees the player bankrupt, and nothing in the sim knows about winning. Rule R-001 makes the sim the only authority on rules, and the AI, the runner and replays must agree on who won and when.
**Decision:** Each company saves its program stage and the tonnes delivered toward the current stage. The world saves one outcome: not ended, or the day, the founder (if any) and each company's result (founded, lesser partner, lost). Program rules live in new sim files, `sim/include/sim/program.h` and `sim/src/program.cpp`. Deliveries and stage completion are orders applied in the commands step, so the first stage-3 completion applied ends the game at once; the deadline is checked at the end of the day. Once the outcome is set, a tick does nothing. Every company's stage, share of deliveries met and stage research are public in every view. The session and client read the outcome from the view; `Session::gameOver` becomes bankrupt or ended.
**Consequences:** The player, the AI, the runner and a replay cannot disagree about the result. A finished game cannot be advanced by any client. Saved state and the played-game golden hash change. A company's program progress is public by rule, which removes any option of hiding it later without changing the view. Same-day finishes are resolved by the order in which commands are applied, which is company order.
