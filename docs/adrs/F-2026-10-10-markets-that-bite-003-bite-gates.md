# ADR-F-2026-10-10-markets-that-bite-003: Bite is gated, mean price is reported

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Status:** Accepted
**Context:** The first aim for this feature was a mean price of 0.7 to 1.3 times base. Exploratory sweeps showed that aim can be met by tripling demand, which also doubles every company's cash: the number passes while the purpose fails. The owner chose to gate on what the player's journey needs instead.
**Decision:** The gated sweep is 50 seeds over 20 years with one rival. It passes only if, for iron, copper and nickel, the median seed has price below base-output break-even on 10 to 50% of days in years 3 to 20; some mine is idled for at least 30 days in at least 40 seeds; company 0's median cash at the end of year 10 is at most half the figure measured once on the pre-008 build; at most one seed has a bankruptcy in years 1 to 10; and the existing opening and leader-over-trailer gates hold. Mean price per ore (aim 0.7 to 1.3; lithium 1.0 to 1.4), bankruptcies in years 11 to 20 and withdrawn land are reported, not gated. The retune is by data only; if the gates cannot all be met that way the build stops and the owner decides which gives way.
**Consequences:** Tuning cannot pass by inflating demand, and a failing sweep says which property of the economy is missing. The cash gate depends on a constant measured once, recorded with its commit in `docs/DESIGN.md`, which must be re-measured if earlier data changes. A 50-seed, 20-year sweep costs test time: release runs it in full under a 60-second ceiling, and the sanitizer preset runs a shorter sweep with the same checks reported but ungated.
