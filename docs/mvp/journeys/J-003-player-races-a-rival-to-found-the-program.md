# J-003 — A player races a rival to found the program

## Actor

The player, founding a mining company in the last decades before the
deadline. They want to be the one whose program succeeds, and to understand
at every moment whether they are ahead.

## Trigger

The first day of a game. The deadline is on screen, and so is a rival with
the same three stages to complete.

## Outcome

They finish first, or lose narrowly, and can say which decisions made the
difference.

## Steps

1. Find and win land, build a first mine, sell ore to get cash moving.
2. Look at what stage one of the program needs and compare it with what
   they can dig.
3. Decide, month by month, how much ore to sell for cash and how much to
   keep back for the program.
4. Start the stage's research when they can afford it.
5. Deliver the ore and pay for the stage. Watch the rival's bar as they do.
6. Go after the ores the next stage needs: new land, new mines, research
   to unlock what they cannot yet dig.
7. Later, build plants to turn ore into the goods the later stages need.
8. Replace mines as they wear out. Borrow when a stage is within reach.
9. Complete the third stage before the rival and before the deadline.

## Failure modes

- They sell everything for cash and find they have nothing to deliver.
- They hoard for the program and run out of money.
- They ignore an ore the last stage needs until the rival holds the land.
- They do not notice the rival pulling ahead.
- They coast on a profitable mine while the clock runs.

## Tools / Systems touched

- A new Program screen
- The Dashboard (deadline and both progress bars)
- Plots, Market, Research and Finance tabs
- The log and its stop rules

## Emotional journey

Pressed for time from the start; torn between cash now and progress;
alarmed when the rival completes a stage; relief or regret at the end.

## Open questions

- How does the player learn what is coming and why (the threat's reveal)?
- When a rival wins, what does being a lesser partner look like?
