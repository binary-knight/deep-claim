# Playtest 2: 2026-10-09

Second monitored test play, and the first on the restyled client. Build
`ba7b53c` (specs 001 to 003 merged), default balance, one AI rival. One
game, seed 229017271121422, played to day 5255 (Y15 M8). Trace
`playtest/trace-20261009-143128.jsonl`, 7,513 records; the replay matched
the client's state hash on every one of 5,255 days.

No screenshots were taken, so the look of the restyle is still unreviewed
by anyone but the player.

## What the last batch fixed

- **Sell policies:** five mines ran for fourteen years with an empty
  warehouse and no storage bill. The failure that ended two of three
  games in playtest 1 did not occur.
- **Auto-pause:** fired on both insolvency warnings, all five auction wins
  and the one loss. The player acted on each.
- **Locked-ore labelling:** no mine was built on locked ore.

## Findings, by weight

### 1. The world runs out and the game has no late game
The world has 30 plots. Three list at the start and one a month, so the
last new plot appeared on day 810. With two mines running, cash stops
being a constraint (about 3M a month against 6.4k a day opex per mine);
after day 1300 nothing forced a decision. Plot count is hard-coded per
region at 10. This is the gap the asteroid phase is meant to fill; until
then the world should be larger, paced by data, and the interface should
say when no unlisted land remains.

### 2. The log is unreadable after year two
1,293 of the log's notices were "went unsold, relisted" and 404 were
monthly auto-sale summaries. Nine unwanted plots relist every 45 days
forever. The player read this as "errors". Relists of plots the player has
no survey or bid on should not be logged, unsold plots should be withdrawn
after a few rounds, and the log needs filters (mine, market, rival, all).

### 3. The opening can offer nothing mineable
After the rival took the nickel plot, every listing until day 90 was Salt
Flats (empty or locked lithium). Listing order is random. Guarantee that
the opening listings include at least two plots with unlocked ore, and
pace regions so one cannot dominate the first year.

### 4. The lithium path dead-ends silently
The tech path to lithium costs over 10M and about three years of lab time.
The rival reached it first and bought every lithium plot 94 days before
the player's research finished; nothing announced the rival's progress.
Rival research milestones should be public notices, and the locked-ore
warning should change to an invitation once the unlocking tech is
researching or one step away ("you can mine this in N days").

### 5. Rival bid premium is learnable but hidden
Rival winning bids were 16%, 16%, 16% and 32% over the ask across both
sessions. The player lost #8 at 11% over. Unchanged from playtest 1: show
recent winning premiums in the plot panel.

### 6. Markets: iron is near break-even, copper is the game
Iron sat at 46 to 56 against a 60 base once both companies sold it; the
fifth mine (iron) added nothing visible to monthly income. Copper fell
from 943 to 605 as the player's two mines and the rival's saturated a
200 t/day market, then recovered. Deposits of 1.59M t copper and 4.4M t
iron make depletion irrelevant at these outputs. Candidates: raise iron
demand or base price, cap deposit sizes, let demand growth matter.

### 7. Interface gaps
- Loans: no "Repay all"; the loan form accepts an amount above the
  remaining credit and does not show or prefill what can be borrowed.
- Research completing does not pause and an idle lab is not in "Needs
  you"; the lab sat idle for months several times.
- Survey complete is off in auto-pause by default; three reports landed at
  5x unnoticed 25 days before an auction closed. Default it on.
- The survey button does not warn when the report will arrive after the
  auction closes, and allows surveying one's own depleted plot.
- Survey estimates overstate small deposits enough that the
  running-low notice never fired for plot #1 (estimate 183.6k t, actual
  173.2k t). Trigger on the lower bound of the estimate.
- Prose: "Couldn't carry out the order for $10.00M: The bank will not
  lend that much on our remaining credit.." (capital mid-sentence, double
  stop); "plot #28 is up for auction" starts lower-case.

### 8. Tooling
- The trace's research orders carry only a tech index; add the id.
- The replay tool prints "building since day N" and "depleted since day
  N" using the opening day; fix the wording.
- The live monitor floods at 20x; the quieter filter (half-yearly state,
  own purchases, mine and research notices) is the one to keep.
