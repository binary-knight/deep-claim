Deep Claim's voice is the operator's desk of a small mining company with a
little in-world flavour: a foreman's note pinned to the board, a radio
call from the survey crew, a month-end line from whoever keeps the books.
Plain, concrete, unhurried. Nouns from the trade: ore, headframe, crew,
footings, the pile, the ledger. Numbers stay exact and are never dressed
up. No buzzwords, no exclamation marks, no corporate register, no jokes
that cost clarity. Short sentences. A reader should know in one glance
what happened and what it costs or earns.
